sphere: optional offline periodic stipple prototype

Unchanged scene scale and white background; black ink; textureScale 1; dot radius 0.3 SVG units. All panels have the same viewBox and display scale. SVG downloads retain their native 900 × 700 dimensions.

Periodic tiles are nested Poisson subsets, not Poisson-disc samples. They reuse halftone tone clips; they are not the production stipple renderer. Repetition, cut dots at geometric boundaries, tone bins, and finite-tile coverage deviations are intentional limitations. No claim of identical appearance or faster rendering.

See STIPPLE-PATTERN-PROTOTYPE.md in the repository for the construction and caveats. This page is standalone/offline; no scripts, fonts, or network assets are loaded.
